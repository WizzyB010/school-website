Privacy Policy

What personal information we collect through this website, why, and how it is protected.

Effective date: 17 September 2026 — this page reflects the actual data practices of wonderheights.edu.gh.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Wonder Heights International School ("Wonder Heights", "we", "us"), Afienya, Greater Accra, Ghana. For any privacy question or request, contact us at admin@wonderheights.edu.gh or 020 101 6051 / 054 579 7990.

2. What we collect

We only collect personal information that you actively choose to submit through a form on this site. Simply browsing the website does not send us any personal data.

  • Enrollment Application form: student first and last name, date of birth, gender, program applied for; parent/guardian name, relationship, country of residence, phone number, email address, and how you heard about us.
  • Newsletter sign-up form: your email address only.

3. Local storage on your device

We store one small, non-tracking value in your browser's local storage (whis-theme) to remember whether you prefer light or dark display mode. It contains no personal data, is never transmitted to us, and is not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind.

4. How your information is used

Information submitted through our forms is used only to process admissions applications, respond to enquiries, and — for newsletter subscribers — send occasional updates about the school. We do not use submitted information for advertising or sell it to any third party.

5. Where your information is sent

Form submissions are sent directly from your browser to a Google Apps Script endpoint and recorded in a Google Sheet that only authorized school staff can access, so that our admissions team can review applications and enquiries. Google LLC acts as our data processor for this storage and is bound by its own privacy policy for the underlying infrastructure. We do not share your information with any other third party.

6. Children's information

Our programmes serve children from 6 months through college age. Where a form concerns a child (for example, the Enrollment Application), it is submitted by that child's parent or legal guardian, who provides consent to this policy on the child's behalf at the time of submission.

7. How long we keep it

We retain application and enquiry data only for as long as necessary for admissions processing, enrolment record-keeping, and legitimate school administration. You may request deletion of your submitted data at any time (see Section 9).

8. Consent notice

On your first visit, this site shows a short notice summarising this policy. Because we do not use tracking or advertising cookies, there is nothing further to "opt out" of — the notice is provided for transparency and so you can review this policy before submitting any form.

9. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have submitted, by emailing admin@wonderheights.edu.gh.

10. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
