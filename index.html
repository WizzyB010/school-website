Our Academic Offerings

Nurturing Every Stage of Growth

From early childhood through to College, every programme is designed to inspire curiosity, build character, and achieve excellence.

Early Sprouts School

Safe, engaging daycare from 7:30am–5pm, guiding toddlers through structured play and guided early-learning activities.

Learn More

Primary School

Integrating the British Primary and GES frameworks to build mastery in Literacy, Numeracy, and Science through inquiry-based learning.

Learn More

Secondary School

Comprehensive preparation for BECE (GES), British Checkpoints, and IGCSE. We bridge national excellence with global qualifications.

Learn More

College

Directing students toward WASSCE, A-Levels, and SAT success. Expert coaching for top-tier university placement in Ghana and abroad.

Learn More

Therapy & Special Needs School

Comprehensive therapeutic support including speech, occupational, and behavioural therapy for children with unique needs.

Learn More

Extracurricular Activities

Art, music, sports, and STEM clubs ensuring every child discovers and develops their unique talents and passions.

Why Choose Wonder Heights

Where Values Meet
Academic Excellence

Our school is grounded in Christian ideals — responsibility, discipline, excellence, ethical behaviour, leadership, and service beyond self.

Early Drop-off & Late Pick-up

We operate from 7:30am to 5:00pm, accommodating working parents with flexible hours and before/after-school care.

Safe, Spacious Environment

Temperature-regulated classrooms, secure perimeter with CCTVs, and trained staff ensure every child is safe and comfortable all day.

Dedicated, Passionate Educators

Our teachers love what they do. Every school day is filled with exciting activities and personalised attention for each learner.

Library & Computer Laboratory

A well-stocked library and modern computer lab give students access to resources that expand their world beyond the classroom.
